Privacy notice

How enquiries through this English-language section are handled.

Contacting isrmtnkv

The enquiry form prepares an email draft in your own email application. You choose whether to send it. The form does not automatically submit your details to a server. You can also contact my@srmtnkv.ru directly or use the linked Telegram account.

Information you choose to share

An enquiry can include your name, email address, website and project details. This information is used to understand your request, reply and discuss a potential service engagement. Avoid including passwords, payment information or sensitive personal data in your message.

External services

Your email provider handles messages you send through it. Telegram handles interactions on its service under its own terms and privacy policy. The hosting infrastructure may process routine connection data needed to serve and protect the website.

Analytics in this section

This English section does not load advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts. Article search operates in your browser; search terms can appear in the page URL. The Russian-language section is a separate version and can use different integrations.

Questions and requests

Contact my@srmtnkv.ru to ask about information you have shared or to request correction or deletion. Information may need to be retained where required for an existing engagement or applicable obligations. This notice was updated on October 19, 2026.
