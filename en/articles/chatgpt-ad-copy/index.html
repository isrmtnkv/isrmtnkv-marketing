ChatGPT ad copy: make the next step useful and specific

A person exploring a problem needs a relevant reason to leave the conversation. Vague brand language often asks for attention without explaining what the destination offers.

The short answer

Write an honest bridge from a customer task to a useful next step. Keep the claim specific and ensure the page fulfills it immediately.

Before you start

  • Confirm current advertiser eligibility and available account capabilities through official documentation and the actual account.
  • Prepare one customer problem, a relevant destination and a clear commercial conversion definition.
  • Assign a limited learning budget and record which evidence would justify extending the experiment.

Put it into practice

01Define the starting point

Choose a concrete problem rather than a broad category slogan. Explain the relevant product or service in ordinary language. Avoid implying that the assistant endorses the advertiser or that the ad controls the answer.

02Build the next step

Use proof that can be checked. If a claim needs important conditions, make those conditions accessible before conversion. A concise message should not depend on a misleading omission.

03Review the evidence

Create a few genuinely different arguments and connect each to a suitable destination. Review enquiry quality, not just the most clickable wording. Broad curiosity can increase visits without improving business fit.

A worked example

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT RESULT

Illustrative example: “Compare implementation options for your support workflow” offers a clear task. “The future of business is here” gives the reader little information about what the click will deliver.

A mistake to avoid

Do not write copy that appears to know someone’s private conversation or sensitive circumstances. Relevance should come from an appropriate offer, not an intrusive claim about the person.

How to evaluate the result

Keep the pilot review focused on your own business evidence. New-channel performance is sensitive to offer, audience, destination and measurement; an early result should not become an industry benchmark. Document what was available in the account when the test ran and recheck change-sensitive claims before the next launch. Evaluate the quality of enquiries alongside delivery and traffic, and allow time for sales progression. Where the evidence is inconclusive, say so and decide whether the value of another learning cycle justifies the expense.

Apply this to your business

Draft three messages with a customer task, concrete benefit, defensible proof and next action. Read each beside its landing page and remove promises the page does not support.

Your working notes

Copy these prompts into your project document. Keep observations separate from assumptions and give the next action an owner.

  1. Eligibility check and current account workflow
  2. Customer hypothesis, message and destination
  3. Pilot outcome, uncertainty and next decision

Record what happened at the next review. If the evidence is incomplete, write down what would resolve the uncertainty instead of forcing a positive or negative verdict.
