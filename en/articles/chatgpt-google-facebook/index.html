ChatGPT Ads, Google Ads or Facebook Ads: compare the job each does

Choosing a channel by trend can distract from the way customers actually discover and evaluate your offer. Each environment creates a different starting point for the message.

The short answer

Compare channels by customer task, available demand, economics and measurement. A channel can complement another without replacing it.

Before you start

  • Confirm current advertiser eligibility and available account capabilities through official documentation and the actual account.
  • Prepare one customer problem, a relevant destination and a clear commercial conversion definition.
  • Assign a limited learning budget and record which evidence would justify extending the experiment.

Put it into practice

01Define the starting point

Map the buying journey before allocating spend. Google Search can capture an explicit query, Facebook and Instagram can introduce an offer during discovery, and ChatGPT Ads can test relevance around an active exploration context where available. These are working roles, not guarantees.

02Build the next step

Use a common business definition of a qualified lead. Keep offers and follow-up comparable where possible, and record the differences you cannot control. Platform conversion totals alone are not a fair cross-channel scorecard.

03Review the evidence

Choose one established channel and one focused experiment when resources are limited. Keep enough attention on creative, pages and sales feedback to learn from both rather than spreading the team thin.

A worked example

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT RESULT

Illustrative example: a specialist consultancy maintains search campaigns for known service demand while testing a narrowly defined educational offer elsewhere. It does not move the entire budget simply because a new inventory source exists.

A mistake to avoid

Avoid comparing a mature campaign’s best historical month with a new channel’s first week. Maturity, seasonality and conversion delay make the comparison misleading.

How to evaluate the result

Keep the pilot review focused on your own business evidence. New-channel performance is sensitive to offer, audience, destination and measurement; an early result should not become an industry benchmark. Document what was available in the account when the test ran and recheck change-sensitive claims before the next launch. Evaluate the quality of enquiries alongside delivery and traffic, and allow time for sales progression. Where the evidence is inconclusive, say so and decide whether the value of another learning cycle justifies the expense.

Apply this to your business

Build a channel matrix listing buyer task, offer, landing page, learning objective, budget cap and the outcome that would justify more investment.

Your working notes

Copy these prompts into your project document. Keep observations separate from assumptions and give the next action an owner.

  1. Eligibility check and current account workflow
  2. Customer hypothesis, message and destination
  3. Pilot outcome, uncertainty and next decision

Record what happened at the next review. If the evidence is incomplete, write down what would resolve the uncertainty instead of forcing a positive or negative verdict.
