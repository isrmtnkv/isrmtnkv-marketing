ChatGPT Ads: plan your first campaign around a business question

A new advertising channel is exciting, but novelty is not evidence of fit. The first useful question is whether it can bring the type of customer your business needs.

The short answer

Start with eligibility, an appropriate offer and a measurable pilot. ChatGPT advertising is separate from organic answers; buying ads is not a way to purchase a recommendation inside an answer.

Before you start

  • Confirm current advertiser eligibility and available account capabilities through official documentation and the actual account.
  • Prepare one customer problem, a relevant destination and a clear commercial conversion definition.
  • Assign a limited learning budget and record which evidence would justify extending the experiment.

Put it into practice

01Define the starting point

Check the official availability information for the legal entity that will advertise and be billed. Account creation does not override country eligibility. Confirm the account’s actual capabilities before building a campaign plan around a specific feature.

02Build the next step

Define a narrow customer problem and a page that resolves it. An ad needs a clear reason to click and a relevant next step. Reuse what you know about customers without assuming search-engine campaign structures transfer unchanged.

03Review the evidence

Specify the pilot’s learning objective and loss limit. Follow visits through enquiry quality and sales progression. Treat early results as evidence from your own setup rather than a universal benchmark for the platform.

A worked example

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO · NOT A CLIENT RESULT

Illustrative example: a software provider tests one use case and one demo page with a limited budget. The goal is to learn whether enquiries fit its ideal customer profile before expanding to every product feature.

A mistake to avoid

Avoid presenting a new channel as guaranteed cheap traffic. Auction conditions, delivery, creative and conversion behavior can change as a platform evolves.

How to evaluate the result

Keep the pilot review focused on your own business evidence. New-channel performance is sensitive to offer, audience, destination and measurement; an early result should not become an industry benchmark. Document what was available in the account when the test ran and recheck change-sensitive claims before the next launch. Evaluate the quality of enquiries alongside delivery and traffic, and allow time for sales progression. Where the evidence is inconclusive, say so and decide whether the value of another learning cycle justifies the expense.

Apply this to your business

Prepare an eligibility checklist, one customer hypothesis, one conversion definition and a pilot review date. Confirm the available measurement workflow before spending.

Your working notes

Copy these prompts into your project document. Keep observations separate from assumptions and give the next action an owner.

  1. Eligibility check and current account workflow
  2. Customer hypothesis, message and destination
  3. Pilot outcome, uncertainty and next decision

Record what happened at the next review. If the evidence is incomplete, write down what would resolve the uncertainty instead of forcing a positive or negative verdict.
